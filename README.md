Sun Palma — an open-world crime game set in a sun-soaked port city: steal cars, outrun the Palma PD, run five story missions and take the city back. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make GTA 6” bounty, $1,666 in USDC.

## What's in the city

- **Open world** — a grid city with a harbour, parks, parking lots and neon rooftops, explored on foot and by car.
- **Traffic and pedestrians** — cars cruise the streets and turn at intersections; pedestrians stroll the sidewalks and flee from danger.
- **Wanted level and police** — crimes raise the heat to five stars. Cruisers chase with intercept steering, pull PIT manoeuvres alongside you, and lay roadblocks across the street at three stars and up.
- **Five story missions** — from stealing your first wheels to a rainy-night getaway in a box truck. Finished jobs can be replayed from the pause menu, and any job can be retried with T.
- **Vehicles** — a sedan, a heavy box truck and a fast coupe, each with its own acceleration, grip and health. Steal any of them with E.
- **Combat** — fists, a pistol, a shotgun and an SMG (keys 1–4 or Q to swap, R to reload), with tracers, muzzle flash and smoking wrecks.
- **Day, night and weather** — a full day/night cycle with streetlights after dark, plus rain storms that soak the roads, slow the traffic and cut your grip, with lightning and thunder.
- **HUD and minimap** — cash, health, car health, wanted stars, clock and weather, the job objective with a distance readout, and a minimap with the route to the objective, roadblocks and police.
- **Start screen and saving** — progress (cash, jobs, unlocked missions, weapon) saves to localStorage; continue from the title screen.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
# or: PORT=9000 node serve.ts
```

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D / arrows | Move / drive |
| Mouse | Look / aim |
| Click | Shoot |
| E | Enter or steal a car |
| Space | Brake / handbrake |
| R | Reload |
| 1–4 / Q | Pick a weapon / cycle weapons |
| T | Retry the current mission |
| Shift | Sprint |
| M | Mute |
| P / Esc | Pause (job board lives here) |
